## Notes on the checks that are deliberately loose

- **Mechanism tolerances are ±3pp, not exact.** The generator applies factors to a
  stochastic process, so realised values differ slightly from the design. Demanding
  an exact match would be a test of arithmetic, not of diagnosis.
- **Two checks are structural, not numeric:** the worst branch and the second-worst
  branch. Those are the claims the findings report actually stakes its recommendations
  on, so they are asserted exactly.
- **One claim is deliberately NOT checked:** why the stock ran out. No data in this
  repository can settle it, and a validation suite that 'passes' an unanswerable
  question would be worse than useless.
