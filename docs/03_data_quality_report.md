## 4. Declared coverage limits (read this before trusting a slice)

1. **Unattributed revenue.** 1,500 line items have no customer identifier. They are legitimately anonymous walk-ins, but it means **KSh 704,943 (0.76% of revenue) cannot be assigned to a customer segment.** Consequence: the segment decomposition does **not** sum to the group total — it reconciles to 99.24%. The analysis states this explicitly rather than hiding it in an 'Other' bucket.
2. **Unmapped SKUs.** 12 lines carry a SKU code that is not in `dim_product`. They are counted in every revenue total but excluded from product-level rankings.
3. **Single-country, single-channel-of-record.** This dataset covers one retailer's POS system. Online orders routed through a marketplace are out of scope.
4. **Simulated data.** The whole dataset is synthetic and its generating mechanism is documented in `docs/00_ground_truth.md`. That is a feature: it lets `src/validate_against_truth.py` check whether the diagnostic recovered the truth.
