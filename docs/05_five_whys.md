## The three parallel chains underneath

Step 3 forks. Each fork is its own short chain with its own owner.

### Fork A — Mombasa Nyali (supply failure)

- **What:** revenue KSh 8.08M -> KSh 5.90M (-27.0%), KSh -2.18M of the group decline (42.2%).
- **How:** customers +7.0%, frequency -11.7%, AOV -22.6%. Lost frequency **and** a smaller basket.
- **Why:** shelf availability for the category's hero SKUs was 9.5% inside the incident window, versus 99.7% elsewhere in the chain.
- **Cost:** KSh 2.16M. Only KSh 0.45M of that is Personal Care revenue (-34.2% in the affected category). The remaining KSh 1.71M is lost baskets in categories that were never short of stock.

### Fork B — Thika Road (store access)

- **What:** revenue -18.7%, KSh 1.50M (29.1% of the group decline).
- **How:** customers +6.9% (900 -> 962) — the store kept and even grew its customer base and still lost 18.7% of revenue.
- **The signature:** visits per customer 5.32 -> 4.06 (-23.7%) while basket value held up (KSh 1,670 -> KSh 1,665, -0.3%). A pure *traffic* problem, not a basket problem — the opposite signature to Mombasa.
- **Cost:** KSh 1.49M.

### Fork C — Beverages (price)

- **What:** category revenue -20.3% (KSh -1.08M) — the steepest category decline in the chain.
- **Why:** discount depth went from 5.79% of gross to 18.85% (+13.05pp). Chain-wide, discount depth rose +3.02pp, giving away an estimated extra KSh 1.43M of gross revenue in the quarter.
- **Cost:** KSh 0.55M of the decline is attributable to Beverages outside the two affected stores.

## The chain-wide backdrop (why the other four stores fell too)

- Baskets got **narrower**: 3.59 lines per trip -> 3.40 (-5.2%), and 5.70 units -> 5.39.
- Prices went **up**, not down: list price per unit KSh 312 -> KSh 326 (+4.3%).
- So AOV fell -4.5% *despite* +4.3% price inflation — that gap is promotion and mix, not price.
- Gross margin compressed from 19.95% to 18.57%, so gross profit fell -16.9% — worse than the revenue headline.
- Retention is intact at the customer level: 83.8% of Q3 2025 customers returned (855 lapsed, 1,145 new). The leak is **how often they come back**, not whether they come back.

## Where this chain stops (and must stop)

- **The residual KSh 0.97M** (18.7% of the decline) sits in 149 cells across the other four stores. Its signature is frequency and basket breadth, not a local event. No single actionable cause is identifiable in this dataset.
- **Kisumu Lakefront** declined -11.5% — material, but with no local event recorded. It is flagged as **unexplained**, not back-filled with a story.
- **Why the SKUs were out of stock** is not answerable here: no supplier, lead-time or replenishment data exists in this extract. That is a data-capture recommendation, not a blank to speculate into.
