# Recommendations Register

> Generated by `src/write_report.py` from `outputs/kpis.json`. Prepared 2026-10-05, following the Q3 2026 close.

Every action carries the four things an action needs to be real (Ch. 24 / Ch. 30): **an owner, an expected impact, an evidence link, and a review date.**

Impacts are stated in **gross profit**, converted from the revenue slice each action recovers at the blended Q3 FY2026 gross margin (18.57%). The pricing action is the exception: a discount is a direct give-away of margin, so clawing it back converts at near-full value rather than at the blended rate. Capture rates (40-100% of each slice) are deliberately conservative.

Taken together the register recovers **KSh 0.85M – KSh 0.97M** of gross profit, about 52-59% of the KSh 1.63M the quarter lost — short of the whole hole on purpose.

## Sequencing and dependencies

1. **Days 0-30:** actions 1 and 4. Both are cheap, reversible, and stop active bleeding. Action 4 is a policy change; action 1 is a purchase order.
2. **Days 30-60:** actions 2 and 3. These need time to show up in traffic data.
3. **Days 60-90:** action 5, which depends on knowing the base is stable first.
4. **Continuous:** action 6. It is the only item that makes the *next* diagnosis cheaper — and this entire report exists because two data gaps had to be worked around.
